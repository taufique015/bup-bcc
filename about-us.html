About
BUP BCC

One club, born of two — and every year since, a little louder about what students here are capable of.

At the close of 2015, a long-discussed merger was finally settled. BUPBC and BUPCC — two clubs that had each built their own following at Bangladesh University of Professionals — amalgamated and became a far stronger single club: the BUP Business & Communication Club.

The combined organisation held on to the principles of both its predecessors and added a few of its own. The experienced students who led it took care to guide the members coming up behind them before they graduated, and that habit stuck. Newer members have been shaped in the mould of the ones before them, and the results show plainly in the simultaneous success of the many events the club organises every year.

“

A preeminent business club for undergraduate students to flourish.

Our purpose is to give the youth of Bangladesh a venue to discover what they are actually capable of — through hands-on, team-based experience rather than theory alone. We push members to be ambitious, and we try to hand them a sense of direction to go with it.

The aspiration is simple enough to say and hard enough to earn: to place BUP prominently in the professional and national arena, and to be known as a club where undergraduates come to flourish.
