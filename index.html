About Us

Bridging Business & Communication

Formed in 2016 through the merger of BUP Business Club and BUP Communication Club — both active since 2010 — BUP BCC has grown into one of the most prestigious and active clubs at Bangladesh University of Professionals. We give students hands-on, team-based experience that sharpens their strengths and builds the confidence to lead in the corporate world.
