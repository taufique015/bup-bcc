About the competition

Where bold ideas become brilliant campaigns

CreADive has spent five editions turning marketing students into working creatives — handing teams a real brief, a tight deadline, and a room full of industry judges. Edition 2026 keeps that same pressure-tested format, with a bigger prize pool, a fresh brief, and a finale judged by some of Dhaka's sharpest creative minds.

Team of 3–5  ·  Open to all universities  ·  2 rounds  ·  Undergrad & postgrad

How it works

From brief to breakthrough

Four rounds separate the good ideas from the ones that actually sell.

Before you register

Eligibility & rules

The essentials — the full rulebook has the fine print.

Who can apply

  • Currently enrolled undergraduate or postgraduate student
  • Teams of 3 to 5 members
  • Members may come from different universities
  • One entry per team

Format & rules

  • Two rounds: concept submission, then a live finale
  • Submit as a PDF deck or a short video pitch
  • Judged on strategy, creativity, and execution
  • Judging panel decisions are final

Questions

Frequently asked

Who is eligible to participate?
Any currently enrolled undergraduate or postgraduate student at a recognized university.
Does it have to be a team, or can I register solo?
Teams only — 3 to 5 members, and they don't need to be from the same university.
Is there a registration fee?
No — CreADive 2026 is free to enter.
What if my team can't attend the finale in person?
The first two rounds are remote, but the grand finale is in-person at BUP Auditorium.
Do all participants get certificates, or just winners?
Every team that submits a concept receives a participation certificate.

Ready to sell your big idea?

Spots are limited — register your team before the brief drops.
